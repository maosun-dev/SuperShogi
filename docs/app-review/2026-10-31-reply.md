# 審査への返信（1.0 の却下 Guideline 2.1 - Information Needed への答え）

使い方：
1. iPhone で画面録画を撮る（下の「録画の撮り方」）
2. App Store Connect → 配信 → App Review → 1.0 の提出 → 「App Reviewに返信」に、下の英文を貼って録画を添付
3. 同じ英文を、バージョンのページの「App Reviewに関する情報」→「メモ」にも貼る

## 録画の撮り方（2〜3分）

最新の iOS の iPhone で、コントロールセンターの「画面収録」を使う。
1. ホーム画面からアプリを起動する（起動の瞬間から撮る）
2. タイトル → 「対局」→ 強さを選ぶ（序幕の「普通」など）
3. 札選び：札を何枚か選んで「対局へ」
4. 対局：駒を数手動かす。札を1枚使う（術札など）。伏せる札（忍札）も1枚使えると良い
5. 王を取るか投了して、結果の画面まで
6. 戻って「札図鑑」「稽古」「設定」を順に少し開く
7. 「友と対局」を開き、近くの相手を探す画面・Game Center でさがす画面を見せる（相手がいなければ探す画面まで）

アカウント登録・ログイン・課金・投稿機能は無いので、それらは撮らなくてよい。

## 返信の英文

Hello App Review team,

Thank you for reviewing Kabuki Shogi. Here is the information you requested. A screen recording captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached.

1. Screen recording
The attached recording starts from launching the app and shows the typical flow: title screen, choosing a CPU difficulty, building a card deck, playing a game against the CPU (moving and promoting pieces, setting a trap card), the result screen, and then the card list and the settings screen. It also shows the "Play with a friend" screens (nearby play and Game Center).
The app has no account registration, no login, no in-app purchases, no paid content and no user-generated content.

2. Purpose and target audience
Kabuki Shogi is a single-player and two-player strategy game based on shogi (Japanese chess), styled after kabuki theater. In addition to normal shogi rules, each player brings a small deck of cards (spells, traps and equipment) that can change the game, such as freezing a piece or setting a hidden pitfall. It is for anyone who likes board games, from shogi beginners (with a tutorial and easy CPU levels) to experienced players (with strong CPU levels). The value it provides is a new, fun and easy-to-start way to enjoy shogi.

3. How to use the main features
No login or setup is needed. Launch the app and tap "対局" (Play) to play against the CPU: choose a difficulty, choose cards, then play. "稽古" (Tutorial) teaches the rules step by step. "札図鑑" (Cards) lists every card. "友と対局" (Play with a friend) lets two people play on two iPhones, either nearby (no internet needed) or online through Game Center.

4. External services
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): banner ads at the bottom of the menu screens. Ads are not shown during a game. The app asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) before loading ads.
- Apple Game Center (GameKit): online matches with friends.
- Apple MultipeerConnectivity: nearby matches between two iPhones. This is local and does not use any server.
The app has no server of its own and does not use AI services, payment processors or other data providers.

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in all regions. The interface is in Japanese.

6. Third-party material
All third-party material is used under its license:
- Music: created by the developer with Suno on a paid plan, which allows commercial use.
- Sound effects: from "Sound Effect Lab" (soundeffect-lab.info) and "On-Jin" (on-jin.com), used according to their terms, which allow use inside apps.
- Fonts: Yuji Boku, Shippori Mincho B1 and Dela Gothic One (SIL Open Font License 1.1), and Aoyagi Kouzan Font T (commercial use permitted).
The credits are shown in the app under Settings > Credits.
